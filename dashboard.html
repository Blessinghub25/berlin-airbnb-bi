Premium Neighbourhood Opportunity
West 5 +267%
At €382/night average, West 5 commands a 267% premium over the Berlin median of €104. For a real estate investor, a property here generates ~€5,551 more estimated monthly revenue than a median Berlin listing.

Market Professionalisation
60.6% multi-listing hosts
Over 60% of Berlin hosts manage more than one listing — this is not a peer-to-peer sharing economy anymore. It signals a professionalised short-term rental market dominated by operators, which has pricing power and occupancy implications for new entrants.

Volume vs. Premium Trade-off
Alexanderplatz — 712 listings
Alexanderplatz has the most listings (712) but at €169/night — only +62.9% vs median. High competition drives pricing down. Lower-volume premium areas like West 5 offer better yield with less competition.
